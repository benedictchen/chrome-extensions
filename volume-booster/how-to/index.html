Amplify any tab's audio beyond Chrome's built-in 100% cap in two clicks.

Step by step

  1. Install Volume Booster from the Chrome Web Store
  2. Navigate to the tab whose audio you want to amplify
  3. Click the Volume Booster toolbar icon
  4. Click Boost and drag the gain slider to the desired level (up to 600%)
  5. Click the icon again or set gain back to 100% to restore normal volume

FAQ

Does this work on YouTube and streaming sites?

Yes — it captures the tab's audio output stream regardless of the site.

Does it collect data about what I watch or hear?

No. All processing is local; nothing leaves your device.

Why doesn't it request access to all websites?

tabCapture + activeTab are sufficient — it only ever touches the one tab you explicitly activate it on.

Will it impact battery or CPU?

The WebAudio gain node is extremely lightweight — negligible overhead.
