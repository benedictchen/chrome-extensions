FAQ

Does it work on every website?

Yes — it applies a dark theme to any site you visit, including ones that have no built-in dark mode.

Can I keep some sites in normal (light) mode?

Yes. The per-site toggle lets you disable dark mode on individual sites without affecting the rest.

Is it free?

Yes, core dark mode and per-site toggle are completely free.
