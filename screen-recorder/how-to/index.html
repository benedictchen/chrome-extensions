Step by step

  1. Click the CleanRec icon in the Chrome toolbar

FAQ

Does it inject scripts into Gmail or Google Drive?

No. Zero content scripts are declared or injected into any page unless you explicitly start a recording on that specific tab.

Where are recordings saved?

Directly to your local Downloads folder — no cloud account or sign-in required.

What permissions does it request?

activeTab, tabCapture, desktopCapture, offscreen, storage, alarms, and notifications — no tabs, webNavigation, or unlimitedStorage.

How large is the extension?

Under 5 MB installed — no vendored SDKs, no debug bundles shipped to production.
